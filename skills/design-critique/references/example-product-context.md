## Review emphasis

For this product, add `Brand and product fit` to the critique when there is enough evidence to assess it.

For `[sensitive-audience]`-facing screens, give additional weight to:

- Clear, reassuring, and actionable content
- Unambiguous next steps and system status
- Low cognitive burden
- Error prevention and recovery
- Readability and visible accessibility risks
- Appropriate trust signals without overstating outcomes

For component or design-system work, give additional weight to:

- Component selection and composition
- Token and spacing consistency
- Variant and state coverage
- Reuse across relevant product surfaces

## Required source material

This file does not contain the product's complete brand principles, copy standards, or design-system specifications. When those materials are available, read them before making compliance claims. Ground every brand, copy, or component-system issue in the supplied source and cite the rule by name when useful.

If the source material is unavailable:

- Assess universal design quality normally.
- Phrase product-specific observations as questions or risks, not violations.
