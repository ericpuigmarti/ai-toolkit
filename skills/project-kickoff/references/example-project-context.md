## Review emphasis

For this product's initiatives, pay particular attention to:

- Existing screens and adjacent flows that establish product patterns
- Conditional states, access states, interruptions, and recovery paths
- Clear next actions, especially in sensitive or high-stakes experiences
- User time, comprehension, trust, and continuity
- Copy-sensitive errors, interruptions, and status messages
- Dependencies on other initiatives, partner timing, policy, or specialist review
- Whether current experience evidence conflicts with the proposed solution

## If current-state screens are unavailable

Ask for them when they are important to the project. If the user chooses to proceed without them, add this risk to the brief in context-appropriate language:

> Current-state screen references were not available. Validate the proposed direction against relevant product and design-system patterns before moving to high fidelity.

## Source discipline

- Prefer the current initiative brief, approved decisions, research, and official design-system documentation over this summary.
- Do not invent brand, copy, privacy, clinical, legal, or policy rules.
- Flag when copy, policy, accessibility, clinical, or other specialist review is needed.
