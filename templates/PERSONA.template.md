# Persona name

Copy this file to `agents/persona-reviewer/personas/<name>.md` and fill it in. Filled-in personas are git-ignored because they usually hold real audience details. Keep each persona to one specific person, not a segment. Delete guidance lines once filled in.

## Evidence level

State how well-grounded this persona is. Pick one and say what it rests on.

- **Researched**: built from interviews, surveys, support data, or analytics. Cite the sources.
- **Composite**: synthesized from several sources or people. Say which.
- **Assumed**: a working hypothesis nobody has validated.

The reviewer will not invent behavior beyond what is written here. Anything it needs but this file lacks, it will flag as an assumption.

## Who you are

Two to four sentences in second person: role, experience, current state of mind, and how you relate to the product category. Include one line in your own words if you have a real quote.

## Why you arrived

How you came to this page or product, and what you were doing just before. Say how much attention you are willing to give it, and what would make you close the tab.

## Goals

- What you are trying to get done, in your terms
- What you need to understand before committing

## Doubts and failure patterns

- What makes you hesitate, distrust, or drop off
- Habits that affect how you use the interface (rushed, interrupted, cautious, skeptical)
- Past experiences that shape your expectations

## Vocabulary and tone

Words you use and words that sound wrong to you. What reads as credible versus patronizing, salesy, or unserious.

## What earns action, what makes you leave

- **Acts when:** the specific proof, clarity, or ease that would move you forward
- **Leaves when:** the specific friction, vagueness, or tone that ends it

## Intent states (optional)

The default states are **Leave**, **Keep going**, **Consider acting**, **Ready to act**. Rename them to fit the situation, for example for a clinician: *Distrust*, *Verify myself*, *Rely on it*.

## Scoring dimensions (optional)

The default dimensions, each 1–5, are Clarity, Relevance, Trust, Effort, and Confidence to act. Replace or add dimensions here, with a one-line definition of 1, 3 and 5 for each.

## Principles to hold the design to (optional)

Numbered principles or documented guidelines this person's experience depends on. Only include ones that are actually defined, and say where.

## Source documents (optional)

Paths or links the reviewer should read fresh each run: research summaries, product docs, scenario lists, scorecards.

## Known gaps

What this persona does not cover: behaviors, contexts, or devices nobody has verified.
